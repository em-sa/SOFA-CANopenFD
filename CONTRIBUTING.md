# Contributing to SOFA

Thank you for your interest in SOFA. Please read this before opening an
issue or pull request — it explains how this project is maintained.

## How this project is maintained

SOFA is developed and maintained **in-house by Embedded Systems Academy
(EmSA)**. It accompanies our CANopen and fieldbus-security work and is
published as a reference and demonstrator. To keep the codebase
consistent and aligned with our internal coding standard and roadmap,
**code is written by EmSA**; we do not generally merge external pull
requests.

This is a deliberate choice about *maintenance model*, not an invitation
to a closed door — see below.

## We welcome your input

Suggestions, corrections, bug reports, and porting feedback are genuinely
appreciated and do influence the project. The best channel is email:

- **opensource@em-sa.com**

Please include enough detail to act on: affected file or document, the
behaviour you saw versus expected, and a minimal reproduction or the
relevant wire sequence where applicable.

You are, of course, free to **fork** SOFA under the terms of the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) and adapt it for your own use.

## Issues and pull requests

- **Issues:** if GitHub Issues are enabled on the repository, you may use
  them for bug reports and questions. Email is equally welcome and is the
  primary channel.
- **Pull requests:** we typically do **not** merge external PRs. If you
  open one, we may close it with a pointer to this document and instead
  consider the idea for an in-house change. Please do not invest
  significant effort in a large PR expecting it to be merged — email us
  first to discuss.

## Security

Do **not** report security issues in public. Follow the private
disclosure process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Licensing of contributions

Any contribution you do send us (by email or otherwise) that we choose to
incorporate is taken in under the project's
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) — inbound license equals outbound license.
By submitting it you confirm you have the right to grant that license.
